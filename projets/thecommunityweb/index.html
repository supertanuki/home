Build a sustainable community

in a place full of mysteries

Settle your community in a charming yet enigmatic place, where every character brings their own skills. Turn it into a self-sufficient eco-hamlet, manage resources while respecting the environment, solve puzzles and uncover buried stories that could well change the course of the adventure.

Challenges abound: limited resources, internal conflicts, the consequences of climate change…

Invent a sustainable future for your residents while unravelling the mysteries of the past.

What is this place hiding?

Behind its apparent calm, the hamlet holds a history and secrets that unfold as you play. A sealed gate at the back of a cellar. A word carved into stone. An unfinished letter slipped into a crack in a wall… Was it once a prison? A monastery?

The characters

Each character has a personal story that you will uncover over the course of the game, and specific skills or know-how that move the story forward.

New characters join the community: single people, couples, families… And others will leave, weary of communal life and of conflicts between residents.

From the creators of Trois-Rivières

Richard Hanna (original idea, code), Philippe Salib (art direction) and David Fonteix (narrative design and music) are the authors of Trois-Rivières, a video game published free online with the support of the French Ministry of Culture and ADEME, the French ecological transition agency.

For The community, they are joined by Christel Médénouvo Yamajako, writer and narrative consultant on inclusion and intercultural issues.
